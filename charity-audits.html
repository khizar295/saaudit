Service Overview

Audits Designed for Charities & NFPs

Registered charities in New Zealand must meet specific reporting obligations under the Charities Act 2005. For many, this includes having their financial statements audited by a qualified auditor. Our charity audit services are specifically designed for the charitable sector — we understand your unique governance structures, funding arrangements, and reporting responsibilities.

We know that charities and NFPs operate differently from commercial businesses. Your revenue comes from grants, donations, and fundraising — not sales. Your stakeholders are funders, members, and the communities you serve. Our audits reflect this understanding, providing assurance that's relevant and proportionate to your size and complexity.

Whether you're a small community charity or a larger charitable organisation with multiple entities, we tailor our approach to deliver a thorough, efficient audit that satisfies Charities Services requirements while being as smooth and straightforward as possible for your team.

Our Process

How Our Charity Audit Works

1

Initial Discussion

We discuss your charity's structure, reporting requirements, and any specific concerns. We identify which entities need auditing and plan the timeline around your filing deadlines.

2

Planning & Proposal

We provide a clear scope, timeline, and fee proposal. For multi-entity charities, we coordinate an efficient schedule to minimise disruption and maximise efficiency across the group.

3

Fieldwork & Review

Our experienced team carries out the audit efficiently, testing your financial records and reviewing your performance report. We work around your availability and keep disruption to a minimum.

4

Audit Report

We deliver your audit report, walk you through the findings, and help improve your performance report. Everything is presented clearly for your board, members, and Charities Services.
