Charity Audit Requirements

Why Charities Need an Audit

Registered charities in New Zealand must meet specific reporting obligations under the Charities Act 2005. Depending on your annual operating expenditure, you may be required to have your financial statements audited or reviewed by a qualified auditor.

Beyond compliance, a quality audit provides assurance to your donors, funders, and community that your charity is managing its resources responsibly and transparently. It strengthens trust and supports your ongoing funding relationships.

Tier 1 & 2 charities — Currently, full audit required for annual expenditure over $5M
Tier 3 charities — Currently, audit or review required for $550K–$1.1M; audit required over $1.1M–$5M. Tier 4 charities — No audit/review required unless constitution mandates (under $550K)
Funder requirements — Many grant providers require audited financial statements
