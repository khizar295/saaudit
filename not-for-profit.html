NFP Audit Requirements

Understanding NFP Audit Obligations

Not-for-profit organisations in New Zealand must comply with specific reporting standards under the Financial Reporting Act and the External Reporting Board (XRB) framework. Whether an audit is required depends on your organisation's size, structure, and governing documents.

Even where an audit isn't legally mandated, many NFPs choose to have one — to demonstrate accountability, satisfy funder requirements, and strengthen governance. A quality audit builds confidence among stakeholders and supports the long-term sustainability of your organisation.

Large NFPs — Full audit required under XRB standards for organisations with annual expenditure over $33M
Medium NFPs — Audit or review may be required by constitution, funders, or legislation
Voluntary audits — Proactive governance decision that builds stakeholder confidence
Our NFP Audit Services

What Our NFP Audits Cover

Financial Statement Audit

Independent audit of your financial statements prepared under NZ-specific accounting standards for public benefit entities, providing assurance to your board, members, and funders.

Review Engagements

Moderate assurance engagements for NFPs that need something less than a full audit but still want credible financial reporting for their stakeholders.

Service Performance Reporting

For NFPs required to report on service performance alongside financial results, we provide assurance over your non-financial achievements and outcomes.

Governance & Controls Review

Assessment of your internal controls and governance framework, providing practical recommendations to strengthen financial management and reduce risk.

Funder Compliance Reporting

Assurance reports tailored to meet specific funder or grant requirements, helping you maintain critical funding relationships and demonstrate responsible stewardship.

Practical Recommendations

Our management letters provide clear, actionable insights that help your board and management improve financial systems, processes, and governance practices.
