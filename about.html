Our Story

Built on Experience & Trust

SA Audit Limited was founded on a simple principle: that quality audit services shouldn't come with unnecessary complexity or expense. Based in Auckland, we've been serving organisations across New Zealand for over two decades.

Our Director, Sheed Ali, trained with reputable Auckland firms before establishing SA Audit to bring Big 4-level expertise with a personal, approachable touch. Today, our experienced team continues that tradition — delivering thorough, independent audits that our clients actually enjoy engaging with.

We specialise in working with small to medium businesses, not-for-profits, charities, and clubs — organisations that deserve the same quality of audit service as larger entities, but with a service approach that's appropriately scaled and genuinely friendly.

Our Values

What Drives Us Every Day

These core values guide every audit engagement and every client relationship.

Integrity & Independence

We maintain the highest standards of professional independence and integrity. Our opinions are objective, unbiased, and based solely on evidence — never influenced by external pressures.

Quality & Excellence

We deliver Big 4-level expertise with rigorous professional standards. Every engagement meets the requirements of International Standards on Auditing (New Zealand) and CA ANZ quality frameworks.

Approachability

We believe audits don't have to be intimidating. Our team brings warmth and clarity to every engagement, explaining findings in plain language and making the process comfortable and straightforward.

Proportionality

We right-size our approach to match your organisation's size and complexity. No over-engineering, no unnecessary procedures — just the right level of scrutiny delivered efficiently.

Community Minded

We're passionate about supporting not-for-profits, charities, and community organisations. We understand your unique challenges and offer proportionate, affordable services that respect your limited resources.

Continuous Improvement

We constantly evolve our methods, embrace modern technology, and share practical insights that help clients strengthen their financial management and internal controls year after year.

Our Purpose

Why SA Audit Was Established

After more than 20 years working with organisations of all sizes, Sheed saw that many businesses, charities and community organisations felt overwhelmed by the audit process. Audits were often viewed as complex, impersonal and disruptive. SA Audit was established to provide a different experience — combining the technical expertise and professional standards of larger firms with a more personal, practical and approachable service. The goal was simple: deliver high-quality audits while building genuine relationships with clients.

Big Firm Quality

Professional standards and technical expertise matching larger firms, delivered with genuine care and attention.

Personal Approach

No cookie-cutter processes. Every engagement is tailored to your organisation's needs and delivered with a friendly touch.

Real Relationships

We don't just deliver reports — we build lasting partnerships based on trust, clarity, and genuine understanding.

With over 20 years of audit experience, Sheed has worked across a wide range of industries — from not-for-profits and charities to SMEs, Māori organisations, and large companies across several sectors.
