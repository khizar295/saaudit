Service Overview

Independent Trust Audit & Assurance

Trusts play an important role in New Zealand's financial landscape — from protecting family wealth to managing Māori land assets. As a trustee, you have legal obligations to ensure the trust's financial affairs are properly managed and reported. A trust audit provides independent assurance that trust financial statements are accurate and compliant.

Our trust audit services cover a wide range of trust structures, including family trusts, Māori trusts, trading trusts, and charitable trusts. We understand the unique requirements and governance considerations that apply to each type, and we tailor our approach accordingly.

Whether your trust deed requires an audit, you're a beneficiary seeking assurance, or you're a trustee who wants to demonstrate sound financial management, our experienced team provides clear, practical audit reporting that trustees and beneficiaries can rely on.

Benefits

Why a Trust Audit Matters

Trustee Compliance

Demonstrate that you're meeting your trustee obligations with an independent audit that confirms the trust's financial statements are accurate and compliant.

Beneficiary Assurance

Provide beneficiaries with confidence that trust assets are being managed properly, distributions are being made correctly, and the trust is performing in their best interests.

Legal Protection

An independent audit can provide trustees with protection by demonstrating they have properly discharged their duties in managing trust affairs.

Tailored to Trust Types

Our Process

How Our Trust Audit Works

A clear, straightforward process from start to finish. We keep you informed at every stage.

1

Initial Discussion

We start with a friendly, no-obligation chat to understand your trust's structure, the trust deed requirements, and any specific concerns. This helps us tailor our approach to your trust's specific needs.

2

Planning & Proposal

We review the trust deed, identify audit requirements, and provide a clear scope, timeline, and fee proposal. You'll know exactly what we'll do, how long it will take, and what it will cost.

3

Fieldwork & Testing

Our experienced team carries out the audit efficiently and thoroughly. We examine trust transactions, verify distributions, review trust investments, and test internal controls with clear communication throughout.

4

Audit Report

We deliver our independent audit report with clear findings and practical recommendations for trustees. We walk you through the results and ensure you understand every detail before finalising.

Trust Audit FAQs

We've answered the questions our clients ask most often. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

Whether your trust needs an audit depends on several factors. Some trust deeds require an audit by the trustee or at the request of beneficiaries. Māori trusts may have audit requirements under Te Ture Whenua Māori Act. Even where an audit is not legally required, many trustees choose to have one to demonstrate sound financial management and protect themselves from potential beneficiary disputes. Contact us for guidance on your specific situation.

A trust audit focuses on the financial statements and operations of a trust structure, while a charity audit is specifically designed to meet Charities Act reporting requirements. Some trusts may also be registered charities and require both types of audit. We are experienced in auditing trusts that are also charities, ensuring both sets of requirements are met efficiently. Learn more about our charity audit services.

The cost of a trust audit depends on the complexity of the trust, the type of trust structure, the condition of financial records, and the specific requirements of the trust deed. We always provide a transparent, upfront fee proposal before any work begins. For a clear, no-obligation quote, get in touch.

Yes. We have experience auditing Māori trusts, incorporations, and community organisations. We understand the unique governance and reporting frameworks that apply and provide culturally aware, respectful audit services tailored to your organisation's needs.

Beneficiaries have the right to request information about trust affairs, and in some cases, an independent audit can be arranged to address their concerns. We work with both trustees and beneficiaries to conduct an objective, impartial audit that provides clarity and confidence to all parties.
