Service Overview

Precision Assurance for Specific Needs

Not every audit fits neatly into standard frameworks. Special purpose audits are designed for situations where financial statements are prepared for a specific purpose — whether required by a regulator, a funding agreement, a contract, or another stakeholder. These audits provide assurance on financial information prepared in accordance with a special purpose framework.

Our special purpose audit services are tailored to your exact requirements. We work closely with you and the intended users of the report to understand the specific framework, criteria, and reporting requirements, ensuring our audit delivers precisely the assurance needed.

As CA ANZ Qualified Auditors, we conduct all special purpose audits in accordance with the applicable New Zealand Standards on Assurance Engagements, maintaining the same rigour and quality that marks all our work.

Common Use Cases

When You Might Need a Special Purpose Audit

Regulatory Requirements

When a regulator or government agency requires an audit on financial statements prepared for a specific purpose or under specific legislation.

Grant Acquittals

When a funding body requires an audit to verify that grant funds have been used in accordance with the grant conditions and reporting requirements.

Contractual Obligations

When contracts, agreements, or trust deeds require financial statements to be audited under a special purpose framework.

Stakeholder Requirements

When specific stakeholders — such as partners, members, or trustees — require financial statements to be prepared and audited for a particular purpose.

Trust & Estate Reporting

When trust deeds require financial statements to be prepared and audited for beneficiaries or trustees under a special purpose framework.

Industry-Specific Reports

When particular industries require financial reporting and audit under specific frameworks, such as construction, property, or financial services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Special Purpose Audit FAQs

A special purpose financial report is prepared for a specific purpose, such as meeting a regulatory requirement, fulfilling a contractual obligation, or reporting to a particular stakeholder group. Unlike general purpose financial statements which are prepared for a broad range of users, special purpose reports are designed for a defined audience and purpose.

A general purpose audit provides an opinion on financial statements prepared in accordance with New Zealand accounting standards for general use. A special purpose audit provides an opinion on financial information prepared under a special purpose framework — for a specific purpose and specific users. The scope and reporting are tailored to the particular requirements.

Special purpose audits are needed by organisations with specific reporting obligations — such as entities required to report to regulators under particular legislation, grant recipients requiring acquittal audits, trusts with special reporting requirements in their trust deeds, and businesses with contractual obligations to provide audited financial information to specific parties.

The cost varies depending on the scope, complexity, and specific requirements of the engagement. We always provide a transparent fee proposal upfront. Because special purpose audits are tailored, the scope is often narrower than a full audit, which can make them more cost-effective. Contact us for a quote.
