Service Overview

Moderate Assurance at the Right Level

A review engagement provides a moderate level of assurance — less than a full audit but more than an agreed-upon procedures report. It's designed for organisations that need to demonstrate financial credibility to stakeholders but don't require, or aren't required to have, a full statutory audit.

In a review, we perform analytical procedures and make enquiries of management, rather than conducting the detailed testing required in an audit. This means we can provide meaningful assurance on your financial statements at a lower cost and with less disruption to your team.

All our review engagements are conducted in accordance with International Standard on Review Engagements (New Zealand) (ISRE 2400), ensuring your review meets the highest professional standards.

Understanding the Difference

Audit vs Review: Which Is Right for You?

Understanding the differences between engagement types helps you choose the right level of assurance for your needs.

Recommended

Review Engagement

Moderate Assurance

  • Analytical procedures and enquiries
  • Negative assurance conclusion
  • Lower cost than a full audit
  • Suitable where statutory audit not required
  • Complies with ISRE 2400 (NZ)

Financial Audit

High Assurance

  • Detailed testing of transactions and balances
  • Positive assurance opinion
  • Higher cost, more extensive procedures
  • Required for many organisations by law
  • Complies with ISAs (NZ)

Agreed-Upon Procedures

No Assurance

  • Specific procedures as agreed
  • Factual findings only — no assurance
  • Most targeted, potentially lowest cost
  • Useful for specific verification needs
  • Complies with ISRS 4400 (NZ)

Who This Is For

When a Review Is the Right Choice

SMEs Not Requiring Audit

Small to medium businesses that want to provide stakeholders with confidence in their financials, but aren't required by law to have a full audit.

Smaller Charities & NFPs

Registered charities and incorporated societies that are not required to have a full audit but want to demonstrate accountability to funders and members.

Clubs & Societies

Sports clubs, community societies, and membership organisations whose constitutions allow a review instead of an audit for member reporting.

Growing Businesses

Businesses that want to build stakeholder confidence and establish a track record of financial credibility ahead of future growth or investment rounds.

Shareholder Reporting

Companies wanting to provide shareholders with meaningful assurance on financial statements without the time and expense of a full statutory audit.

Regulatory Flexibility

Organisations where legislation or constitutions permit a review instead of an audit, providing compliance with reduced cost and disruption.

Benefits

Why Choose a Review Engagement

More Affordable

Reviews typically cost significantly less than a full audit because they involve fewer procedures, less detailed testing, and a shorter timeframe.

Faster Completion

With a narrower scope, review engagements are typically completed more quickly than audits, reducing disruption to your operations.

Meaningful Assurance

A review provides a moderate level of assurance that stakeholders can rely on — more credible than no assurance at all, and sufficient for many purposes.

Professional Standards

All reviews are conducted in accordance with ISRE 2400 (NZ), ensuring your engagement meets the required professional and ethical standards.

Less Disruptive

Reviews involve fewer requests for documentation and less time on-site, making the process smoother and less disruptive to your day-to-day operations.

Stakeholder Confidence

A review from a CA ANZ Qualified Auditor gives funders, members, and other stakeholders confidence in your financial reporting — even without a full audit opinion.
