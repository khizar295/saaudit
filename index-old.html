About Us

Auckland's Trusted Audit Partner

SA Audit Limited is an Auckland-based audit firm providing quality assurance services across New Zealand. As Qualified Auditors of Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ), we bring professional excellence to every engagement.

For over 20 years, we have been serving clients of all sizes — particularly small to medium businesses, not-for-profits, charities, and clubs. Our approach is simple: provide thorough, independent audit services with a friendly, personal touch that makes the process as smooth as possible.

Our Services

Audit & Assurance Services in Auckland

We offer a full range of audit and assurance services tailored to the unique needs of your organisation, whatever its size or sector.

Financial Audits

Independent statutory audits that provide assurance on your financial statements, meeting regulatory requirements and stakeholder expectations.

Review Engagements

A moderate level of assurance for organisations that don't require a full audit. We assess your financials with analytical procedures and enquiries.

Agreed-Upon Procedures

Targeted procedures carried out on specific subject matter as agreed with you. Factual findings reported without an assurance opinion.

Related Services

Internal audit support, compliance reviews, and specialist assurance services designed around your unique requirements and governance needs.

Need something specific? We can help.

Whether you need a statutory audit, a review engagement, or specialised agreed-upon procedures, we tailor our services to meet your specific requirements. Our experienced team works alongside you to deliver clear, actionable insights.

Our Team

Led by Experience & Dedication

Sheed Ali

Director · Chartered Accountant · CA ANZ Qualified Auditor

Sheed Ali is the Director of SA Audit Limited and a Chartered Accountant with CA ANZ Qualified Auditor status. With over 20 years of audit experience, Sheed has worked across a wide range of industries — from not-for-profits and charities to SMEs, Māori organisations, and large companies across several sectors.

Trained in reputable New Zealand firms, Sheed brings Big 4-level expertise and methodology to every engagement. His background includes auditing organisations of all sizes and complexity, giving him a deep understanding of the challenges each sector faces.

After more than 20 years working with organisations of all sizes, Sheed saw that many businesses, charities and community organisations felt overwhelmed by the audit process. Audits were often viewed as complex, impersonal and disruptive. SA Audit was established to provide a different experience — combining the technical expertise and professional standards of larger firms with a more personal, practical and approachable service. The goal was simple: deliver high-quality audits while building genuine relationships with clients.

Sheed leads a friendly and experienced audit team dedicated to providing clear, practical, and professional assurance services. Whether you're a small business owner, a charity treasurer, or a club committee member, Sheed and the team ensure your audit experience is straightforward and stress-free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Audits

We've answered the questions our clients ask most often. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

Audit fees depend on the size and complexity of your organisation, the type of engagement required, and the condition of your financial records. We always provide a transparent fee proposal upfront before any work begins, so there are no surprises. For a clear, no-obligation quote, get in touch — we're happy to discuss your needs.

The timeframe varies based on the size and complexity of your organisation. A straightforward audit may take 2–4 weeks from fieldwork to final report, while more complex engagements can take longer. We always agree on a timeline upfront and keep you informed throughout the process. Our priority is completing your audit efficiently without compromising quality.

It depends on your regulatory requirements, stakeholder expectations, and the level of assurance needed. A financial audit provides a high level of assurance and is required for many organisations. A review engagement provides moderate assurance and can be suitable where a full audit isn't mandated. We can help you determine which is right for your situation — contact us for guidance.

We'll provide a tailored list of documents at the start of the engagement. Typically this includes financial statements, bank statements, reconciliations, board minutes, contracts, and other supporting records. The earlier these are prepared, the smoother and faster the audit process. We always guide you through what's needed so there are no surprises.

Yes, we can conduct much of the audit process remotely. We use secure digital tools for document sharing, virtual meetings, and communication. While some on-site work may be beneficial depending on the engagement, we're experienced in delivering audits efficiently whether in person or remotely — particularly for organisations across New Zealand.
