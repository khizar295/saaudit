Service Overview

Strengthening Your Internal Systems

Internal audit is a vital function that helps organisations evaluate the effectiveness of their internal controls, risk management processes, and governance frameworks. However, many small to medium organisations don't have the resources or need for a full-time internal audit team.

Our internal audit support services provide you with access to experienced, CA ANZ-qualified professionals who can review your processes, identify control weaknesses, and provide practical recommendations for improvement — all without the overhead of an in-house team.

Whether you need a one-off internal control review, ongoing internal audit support, or help preparing for your external audit, our team works alongside you to strengthen your governance and operational effectiveness.

What We Offer

Internal Audit Support Services

Internal Control Reviews

Evaluate the design and effectiveness of your internal controls, identify weaknesses, and receive practical recommendations for strengthening your control environment.

Risk Assessment

Identify, assess, and prioritise the key risks facing your organisation, and develop strategies to mitigate them effectively.

Compliance Reviews

Assess your compliance with relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies, and provide recommendations to address any gaps identified.

External Audit Prep

Prepare for your external audit by reviewing documentation, testing key controls, and ensuring your records are audit-ready — saving time and reducing the cost of your external audit.

Process Improvement

Review your financial and operational processes for efficiency and effectiveness, and recommend improvements that save time and reduce risk.

Ongoing Support

Regular internal audit support for organisations that need ongoing monitoring but don't require a full-time internal auditor. We become your trusted internal audit partner.

An external audit provides an independent opinion on your financial statements for stakeholders. Internal audit focuses on evaluating and improving your organisation's internal controls, risk management, and governance processes. They serve different purposes but complement each other — strong internal controls make your external audit smoother and more efficient.

Not every organisation needs a full-time internal audit function. However, most organisations benefit from periodically reviewing their internal controls and risk management. Our internal audit support services give you access to professional internal audit expertise without the cost of a full-time employee.
