Service Overview

Independent Due Diligence You Can Rely On

Whether you're buying a business, selling, entering a partnership, or making a significant investment, you need to understand exactly what you're dealing with. Financial due diligence provides an independent, thorough examination of the financial aspects of a transaction — so you can proceed with confidence.

Our due diligence services go beyond the numbers. We analyse financial performance, assess risks, verify key assumptions, and identify issues that could affect the value or viability of the transaction. Our experienced team brings both technical rigour and commercial awareness, providing you with clear, actionable insights.

Our work is particularly valuable for clients who have used our agreed-upon procedures engagement for FMCG businesses facing delisting risk — demonstrating the depth of commercial insight we bring to transaction-based assurance work.

What We Examine

Key Areas of Focus

Financial Performance

Analysis of revenue, expenses, profitability, and trends to verify the financial performance being presented.

Balance Sheet Verification

Verification of key balance sheet items including assets, liabilities, bank balances, receivables, and payables.

Risk Identification

Identification of financial risks, contingent liabilities, and potential deal-breakers that could affect the transaction.

Working Capital

Assessment of working capital requirements, cash flow sustainability, and the quality of earnings being presented.

Compliance Review

Review of tax compliance, regulatory requirements, and any outstanding disputes or legal matters affecting the business.

Commercial Assessment

Evaluation of the commercial viability of the transaction, including market positioning, customer concentration, and growth assumptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Due Diligence FAQs

Common questions about our financial due diligence services.

Financial due diligence is an independent examination of a target business's financial information before a transaction such as an acquisition, merger, or investment. It involves analysing financial performance, verifying assets and liabilities, identifying risks, and assessing the commercial viability of the transaction to help you make informed decisions with confidence.

You need due diligence when buying or selling a business, entering a partnership or joint venture, making a significant investment, or entering major supply or distribution agreements. Any transaction where you need independent verification of financial information to proceed with confidence is a candidate for due diligence.

The timeframe depends on the complexity of the transaction and the condition of the target's financial records. A straightforward due diligence engagement may take 2–4 weeks, while more complex transactions can take longer. We work to your timeline and can mobilise quickly when timeframes are tight.

An audit provides an opinion on whether financial statements give a true and fair view, conducted in accordance with auditing standards. Due diligence is a broader examination focused on a specific transaction — it goes beyond the numbers to assess risks, verify key assumptions, and provide commercial insights. Due diligence is tailored to the transaction, while an audit follows a standard framework.

The cost depends on the scope, complexity, and specific requirements of the engagement. We always provide a transparent fee proposal upfront before any work begins. Contact us to discuss your transaction and we'll provide a tailored quote.
