Service Overview

Focused, Targeted Assurance

An Agreed-Upon Procedures (AUP) engagement is different from an audit or review. Instead of providing an opinion or conclusion, we carry out specific procedures that you and the intended users agree upon, and report the factual findings. This makes AUP engagements highly flexible — you get exactly the level of scrutiny you need, focused on the areas that matter most.

AUP engagements are ideal when stakeholders need reliable information about specific aspects of your financial data, but don't require a full audit opinion. They're commonly used for grant acquittals, regulatory reporting, vendor compliance, and situations where specific verification is needed quickly and cost-effectively.

As CA ANZ Qualified Auditors, we conduct all AUP engagements in accordance with the relevant New Zealand Standards on Assurance Engagements, ensuring your findings are credible, reliable, and accepted by the parties that need them.

Who This Is For

When AUP Engagements Are the Right Choice

Businesses & FMCG

Companies needing specific financial data verified for commercial purposes — such as demonstrating financial viability to retailers, suppliers, or investors.

Funders & Grant Recipients

Organisations that need to acquit grants or demonstrate to funders that specific conditions have been met with independently verified findings.

Shareholders & Partners

Business partners or shareholders requiring independent verification of specific financial data as part of partnership agreements or shareholder requirements.

Regulatory Compliance

Organisations that need to meet specific regulatory requirements or demonstrate compliance with particular conditions to government agencies or regulators.

Contractual Obligations

Where contracts or agreements require independent verification of certain financial data, AUP engagements provide the factual findings without the cost of a full audit.

Charities & NFPs

Charities needing to verify specific financial data for grant acquittals, donor reporting, or to satisfy particular funder requirements without a full audit.

Benefits

Why Choose Agreed-Upon Procedures

Targeted & Focused

Only the procedures you need — no unnecessary work. We focus on the specific areas that matter to you and your stakeholders.

Cost-Effective

Because we only perform the agreed procedures, AUP engagements are typically more affordable than a full audit while still providing independent, credible findings.

Faster Turnaround

With a narrower scope, AUP engagements can often be completed more quickly than a full audit — ideal when timeframes are tight.

Independent & Credible

As CA ANZ Qualified Auditors, our findings carry credibility with regulators, funders, and business partners — giving stakeholders confidence in the data.

Flexible Scope

The scope is entirely up to you and the intended users. We can verify revenue, expenses, bank balances, compliance metrics, or any other specific subject matter.

Clear Reporting

Our reports present factual findings clearly and transparently, making it easy for all parties to understand exactly what was tested and what was found.

Our Process

How Our AUP Engagement Works

1

Define Procedures

We work with you and the intended users to agree on the specific procedures to be performed. This ensures everyone understands what will be tested and reported.

2

Plan & Execute

Our experienced team carries out the agreed procedures on the specified subject matter. We work efficiently and keep you informed throughout the process.

3

Report Findings

We prepare a comprehensive report of factual findings based on the procedures performed. The report is clear, practical, and tailored to the needs of the intended users.

4

Review & Discuss

We walk you through the findings and discuss any implications. Our goal is to ensure you understand the results and can act on them with confidence.
