Incorporated Society Audit Requirements

Why Incorporated Societies Need an Audit

The Incorporated Societies Act 2022 introduced significant changes for New Zealand's approximately 24,000 incorporated societies. Under the new Act, many societies are now required to have their financial statements audited or reviewed by a qualified auditor, depending on their annual operating expenditure.

Even where an audit isn't legally required, your society's constitution may mandate one — or your members may expect it as a matter of good governance. An independent audit provides accountability to members and confidence that society funds are being managed responsibly.

Large societies — Audit required for societies with annual operating expenditure over $1M
Medium societies — Audit or review required for expenditure between $500K–$1M
Constitutional requirements — Many society rules mandate audits regardless of size
Member confidence — Independent assurance that funds are managed responsibly
