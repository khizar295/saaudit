Charity & NFP Case Study

How We Helped: Charities Managing Urgent Audit Deadlines

A charitable group supporting Māori and Pacific communities faced audits more than 18 months behind schedule. We mobilised rapidly and completed six entity audits in approximately three months — restoring good standing and confidence.

Rapid Mobilisation Multi-Entity Audit Charity & NFP
The Situation

An Audit Crisis

A charitable group supporting Māori and Pacific communities faced significant audit delays. Audits for the previous two years had fallen more than 18 months behind schedule, creating a backlog and putting immense pressure on the current year's audit.

The group operated across multiple entities — including media broadcasting, community support services, rehabilitation programmes, family assistance, and education initiatives — adding significant complexity to the audit process.

They needed a team that could mobilise quickly, work across complex structures, and deliver quality under extreme time pressure.

The Challenges

What Stood in the Way

Severe Delays

Prior-year audits over 1.5 years behind schedule

Compressed Timeline

Current audit cycle under extreme time pressure

Multiple Entities

Complex group structure across six different entities

Public Scrutiny

High reporting expectations due to public accountability

Non-Compliance Risk

Risk of non-compliance with filing authorities

What We Did

Our Response

1

Rapid Audit Mobilisation

  • Deployed an experienced audit team immediately
  • Prioritised planning and coordination across all entities
  • Streamlined communication with management and stakeholders
2

Multi-Entity Execution

  • Completed audits for six entities within approximately three months
  • Significantly reduced turnaround time compared to the previous nine-month cycle
  • Maintained audit quality while accelerating delivery
3

Enhanced Reporting

  • Reviewed and improved the performance report
  • Ensured alignment with best-practice and high-quality reporting standards
  • Added clarity and consistency across the group's reporting
The Result

From Crisis to Confidence

Audits Completed

All audits completed within a significantly shortened timeframe

Good Standing Restored

Charity restored to good standing with filing authorities

Confidence Gained

Board and staff gained confidence in timely, reliable reporting

Quality Reporting

Performance reporting elevated to a high standard
