FMCG Business Case Study

How We Helped: FMCG Business Facing Supermarket Delisting Risk

An established FMCG business was at risk of losing its supermarket shelf space entirely. Our independent assurance helped them demonstrate commercial viability — and retain the relationships that kept their business alive.

Agreed-Upon Procedures Investor Confidence FMCG & Retail
The Situation

A Business at the Brink

An established FMCG business with decades of operating history relied heavily on major supermarket chains for revenue. As part of a large-scale supermarket restructuring, suppliers were required to re-compete for shelf space.

Despite a long-standing presence, the client was at risk of being removed entirely and replaced by larger competitors within a short timeframe. This posed an immediate threat to the survival of the business.

They needed credible, independent verification of their financial position — and they needed it fast.

The Challenges

What They Were Up Against

Revenue Dependence

Heavy reliance on major supermarkets for the majority of revenue

Competitive Tendering

Forced re-tendering for shelf space against larger competitors

Tight Timeframe

Only 6–12 months to demonstrate commercial viability

Investor Uncertainty

Lack of independently verified financial information eroded confidence

Existential Risk

Risk of significant revenue loss or even business closure

What We Did

Our Approach

Independent Assurance

Agreed-Upon Procedures

  • Conducted detailed agreed-upon procedures to provide credibility to financial and operational data
  • Reviewed and validated stock levels and inventory management
  • Analysed pricing structures and product margins
  • Verified sales and purchase transactions
  • Confirmed key expense categories, including subcontractor costs

Strengthened Investor Confidence

Assurance & Reporting

  • Provided assurance over critical balance sheet items, including bank balances, receivables, and payables
  • Delivered clear, structured reporting to support investor decision-making
  • Helped position the business as stable, transparent, and commercially viable
The Result

A Business Preserved

Shelf Space Retained

The client retained shelf space with major supermarkets despite the restructuring

Supply Maintained

Continued supply relationships with key retailers were preserved

Revenue Protected

Ongoing operations and revenue streams were maintained

Value Preserved

Long-term value for owners and investors was protected
