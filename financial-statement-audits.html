Service Overview

Independent Assurance You Can Trust

A financial statement audit is the highest level of assurance engagement. Our independent auditors examine your financial statements in accordance with New Zealand auditing standards, providing stakeholders with confidence in the accuracy and completeness of your financial reporting.

Whether you're a business requiring a statutory audit, a charity meeting Charities Act reporting obligations, or a not-for-profit seeking to demonstrate accountability to funders and members, our audits are conducted with rigour, integrity, and a personal approach that makes the process straightforward.

We comply fully with International Standards on Auditing (New Zealand) and the professional requirements of Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand (CA ANZ), ensuring your audit meets the highest professional benchmarks.

Who This Is For

Organisations That Benefit from a Financial Audit

Small & Medium Businesses

Companies required by law to have a statutory audit, or those choosing to strengthen stakeholder confidence with audited financial statements.

Charities & NFPs

Registered charities and not-for-profit organisations needing to meet Charities Act reporting requirements and demonstrate accountability to funders.

Trusts

Family trusts, Māori trusts, and other trust structures requiring independent assurance over trust financial statements for trustees and beneficiaries.

Clubs & Societies

Sports clubs, community societies, and membership organisations needing audits to meet constitutional or regulatory requirements.

Larger Companies

Companies that exceed the Financial Reporting Act thresholds and are required to have their financial statements audited by a qualified auditor.

Government-Funded Bodies

Organisations receiving government funding that require audited financial statements as a condition of their funding agreements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Audits

We've answered the questions our clients ask most often. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

A financial statement audit is an independent examination of your financial statements conducted in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (New Zealand). The auditor provides an opinion on whether the financial statements present a true and fair view of the organisation's financial position and performance. It is the highest level of assurance available.

This depends on your organisation type, size, and regulatory requirements. Companies exceeding Financial Reporting Act thresholds, registered charities with expenditure over a certain level, and many incorporated societies require audits by law. Even when not legally required, many organisations choose to have a financial audit to strengthen stakeholder confidence and improve internal controls. Contact us for guidance on your specific situation.

Audit fees depend on the size and complexity of your organisation, the type of engagement required, and the condition of your financial records. We always provide a transparent fee proposal upfront before any work begins — no hidden costs. For a clear, no-obligation quote, get in touch.

The timeframe varies based on the size and complexity of your organisation and the readiness of your records. A straightforward audit may take 2–4 weeks from fieldwork to final report, while more complex engagements can take longer. We always agree on a timeline upfront and keep you informed throughout the process.

A financial audit provides a high level of assurance — the auditor gives an opinion on whether the financial statements are true and fair. A review engagement provides moderate assurance — the auditor performs analytical procedures and enquiries but does not conduct detailed testing. An audit is more thorough and is required for many organisations, while a review may be suitable where a full audit is not mandated. Learn more about reviews.
