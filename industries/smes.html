Why SMEs Need an Audit

While not all small to medium enterprises are legally required to have an audit, many choose to — or are required to by lenders, shareholders, or their constitution. An independent audit adds credibility to your financial statements and provides assurance to those who rely on them.

For SMEs with bank lending, external shareholders, or growth ambitions, an audit is often a necessary step. The key is ensuring the audit is proportionate to your size and complexity — which is exactly what we deliver.

Lender requirements — Banks often require audited financial statements as a condition of lending
Shareholder assurance — Minority or external shareholders may require independent audit
Large companies — Certain large companies and FMC reporting entities may be required to prepare and file audited financial statements
Voluntary audits — Proactive governance that builds credibility with suppliers, customers, and partners
Our SME Audit Services

What Our SME Audits Cover

Financial Statement Audit

Independent audit of your financial statements under NZ accounting standards, providing assurance for lenders, shareholders, and other stakeholders.

Review Engagements

Moderate assurance for SMEs that don't need a full audit but require credible financial reporting for lenders or shareholders.

Agreed-Upon Procedures

Targeted procedures on specific areas — revenue verification, expense testing, debtor confirmation — factual findings without a full audit opinion.

Lender Compliance

Assurance reports tailored to meet specific bank or lender requirements, helping you maintain lending facilities and covenant compliance.

Internal Controls Review

Assessment of your financial controls and processes, providing practical recommendations to strengthen governance and reduce the risk of error or fraud.

Practical Recommendations

Management insights on profitability, cash flow management, and financial controls — helping you run a better business, not just tick a compliance box.
