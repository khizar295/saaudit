Why Charities Need Specialist Audit

Charity Audits Are Different

Charitable organisations operate under a unique set of regulations, stakeholder expectations, and governance structures. The Charities Act 2005 imposes specific reporting obligations on registered charities, and understanding these requirements is essential for any auditor working in the sector.

At SA Audit, we don't just apply a standard audit template to every charity. We understand that a small community foodbank has different audit needs to a large charitable trust managing significant assets. Our approach is proportionate — thorough enough to provide real assurance, but efficient enough to respect your limited resources.

Our Director, Sheed Ali, has extensive experience auditing charities of all sizes, from small community organisations to large multi-entity charitable groups. This depth of sector knowledge ensures your audit is conducted by people who truly understand your world.

Charity Audit Requirements

Understanding Your Reporting Obligations

Registered charities in New Zealand must meet specific reporting obligations under the Charities Act. Here's what you need to know.

Most Common

Financial Audit

Charities Act Requirement

Currently, charities with annual operating expenditure over $5 million must have their financial statements audited by a qualified auditor. For expenditure between $550,000 and $1.1m, an audit or review is required. Over $1.1m to $5m, an audit is required.

Even charities below the threshold often choose to have an audit to demonstrate accountability to funders, members, and Charities Services.

Review Engagement

Lower Assurance

Some smaller charities may be permitted to have a review instead of an audit, depending on their size and constitutional requirements. A review provides moderate assurance at a lower cost.

We can advise you on whether a review or audit is appropriate for your charity.

Important: Performance Report

All registered charities must prepare a performance report as part of their annual financial statements under NZ accounting standards. We review and improve your performance report as part of our audit to ensure it meets best-practice standards.
